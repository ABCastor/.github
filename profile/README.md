We build modular open software, most of it for working alongside AI agents, and we write about how people and machines work together at [abcastor.com](https://abcastor.com/writing/).

### Works

In each group, the ones we use most come first.

**For AI agents**

- **[Gaddi](https://github.com/ABCastor/gaddi)** lets AI agents work in your own Chrome tabs, with your logins, and holds anything you cannot take back for your approval.
- **[OmniRead](https://github.com/ABCastor/omniread)** turns a web page into Markdown for AI agents and says, with evidence, whether it got all of it.
- **[VerifyLab](https://github.com/ABCastor/verifylab)** keeps the proofs, checks and reviews behind AI research in plain files that anyone can re-run.
- **[Crossfeed Orchestrator](https://github.com/ABCastor/crossfeed-orchestrator)** routes AI coding work across the subscriptions you already pay for.
- **[Melzi](https://github.com/ABCastor/melzi)** gives AI coding tools design review skills, so the interfaces they build stop looking machine-made.

**For your own data**

- **[SheetOps](https://github.com/ABCastor/SheetOps)** is a command line for Google Sheets and Apps Script where every change is a patch you preview and confirm.
- **[Findstill](https://github.com/ABCastor/findstill)** searches your Apple Photos by a description of the picture, on your own Mac.
- **[publishable](https://github.com/Anneo22/publishable)** checks a repository's full history for the personal data that secret scanners miss.
- **[GarminBridge](https://github.com/Anneo22/garminbridge)** copies a Garmin watch's voice notes and activities to a Mac on its own.
